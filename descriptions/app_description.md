**App Description:**
The app connects entrepreneurs with investors by allowing entrepreneurs to describe their company and target audience, while investors register with validation. Admins facilitate the connection between the two groups.

**Target Audience:**
The primary users are startups and scientific researchers with project ideas, as well as investors who have no predefined characteristics. Admins are also involved to help facilitate connections.

**User Needs:**
The app addresses the issue of communication by implementing structured questionnaires, ensuring that entrepreneurs provide clear and comprehensive information, streamlining communication with investors.

**Core Features:**
1. Admins can create structured questionnaires using JSON.
2. Entrepreneurs can fill out forms that are designed to be visually appealing.
3. Targeted questionnaires focus on entrepreneurs' startups and desired investor types.

**Tone and Style:**
The app should strike a balance between a professional and casual tone, making it approachable yet sophisticated.

**Visual Design:**
The design will be minimalistic, featuring non-vivid, pleasant color tones to create a clean and user-friendly interface.

**Business Model:**
Revenue will be generated through a subscription model, priced at $15/month.
